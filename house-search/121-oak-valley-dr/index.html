Problems and risks

Lower-level finish not on the county record

The county lists the basement as unfinished and the house as 3 bedrooms. Ask whether permits were pulled and whether the lower bedroom has a code-size escape window.

Cost: If unpermitted, the fix ranges from a few hundred for an inspection sign-off to a few thousand for an egress window (estimate).

Taxes will rise after the sale

The county dropped its value to $184,355 for 2025. A sale near $313,000 gives the assessor a new number to work from at the 2027 reassessment.

Cost: Today $2,169 a year. At the county's current rate on a $313,000 value it would be roughly $3,700 (estimate).

Two bathrooms for four bedrooms

Both bathrooms are on the main floor; the lower-level bedroom has none on its floor.

Cost: Adding a lower-level bath runs $12,000 to $25,000 (estimate).

Drive time

19 minutes at midday. Rush hour on Highway K or Interstate 70 will push it past 20 some days.

Cost: Time, not money.
