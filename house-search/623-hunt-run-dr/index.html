Problems and risks

Four years of tenants

New floors and paint cover wear. Check under sinks, around the tub and in the basement for old leaks, and test every appliance.

Cost: Inspection about $400 to $600.

Association fee typo

$28,500 a year cannot be right for a subdivision that only maintains common ground. Get the real fee and the covenants in writing.

Cost: Probably a few hundred a year once confirmed.

Original 2009 mechanicals

Rental companies rarely replace a working furnace. Ask for data-plate photos.

Cost: $9,500 to $16,500 if all are original and due (estimate).

Finishing the basement later

The rough-in makes a third bath easy, and the walkout allows legal bedrooms.

Cost: Finishing 1,000 to 1,500 square feet runs $35,000 to $70,000 (estimate).

Ask before making an offer

  1. Real association fee and what it covers.
  2. Ages of the furnace, air conditioner, water heater and roof.
  3. Is it vacant now, and when did the last tenant leave?
  4. Any maintenance records from the rental company?
