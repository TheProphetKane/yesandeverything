How these houses were found

Searched October 27, 2026, twice. First on Zillow: every house listed for sale within about 13 miles of the daycare, asking $335,000 or less, with 3 or more bedrooms and 1,600 or more square feet. 61 matched, 31 of them new-construction floor plans with no history to report, so they are left out. That pass kept the existing houses with 4 or more bedrooms and a drive under 20 minutes, plus 17 Savoy Dr, a 3-bedroom in the same Lake Saint Louis association as Cadillac Ct. Then the same evening against the MLS feed, read through Realtor.com and Redfin, for all of St. Charles County under the same filters: 139 listings, 92 of them existing single-family houses. Every one was routed to the daycare, and the ones under 20 minutes were read for the two must-haves. That added six houses, all 3-bedroom ranches or splits the Zillow pass had skipped by asking for 4 bedrooms. Left out on purpose: 2451 Fox Bridge Ct, an attached villa with a $358 monthly association fee; 6 Westwood Estates Dr, a 24-minute drive; 500 Flowering Magnolia Dr, an attached villa with a $230 monthly fee and its bedrooms upstairs; 8 Roland Ln, with only 2 bedrooms on the main floor; and 37 Royal Oaks Dr, added from the feed and taken off the same evening when Google Maps put it at 22 minutes for noon on a Tuesday (typically 18 to 30). Drive times for the first nine are Google Maps read live at midday on a Tuesday. The six added from the feed were run in Google Maps that evening for noon the following Tuesday, which gives a typical range, and the page shows Google's headline figure.
