Problems and risks

Original furnace, air conditioner and water heater

The utility room photos show older equipment (photos 34 to 36). A 2001 furnace and air conditioner are 25 years old.

Cost: $8,000 to $14,000 for both, $1,500 to $2,500 for the water heater (estimates).

The contract that fell through

Under contract September 28, back October 7. Ask the listing agent what the buyer's inspection found.

Cost: Unknown until asked.

Fresh-flip finishes can hide problems

New paint and flooring everywhere make old stains and patches invisible. Look closely at ceilings under bathrooms and at the lower-level walls.

Cost: Inspection, about $400 to $600.

Smaller living space

1,662 square feet with the fourth bedroom on the lower level. The recreation room is the only second living space.

Cost: No fix short of an addition.

Ask before making an offer

  1. Why did the September contract fall through?
  2. Ages of the furnace, air conditioner, water heater and roof.
  3. Who did the updates, and were permits pulled for the bathroom work?
  4. What radon level was measured, and when was the fan installed?
