Problems and risks

Two failed contracts in two weeks

October 11 to 13, then October 16 to 24. Back-to-back collapses usually mean an inspection finding or an appraisal that came in low. Get the reason in writing before paying for an inspection.

Cost: Unknown until asked.

Plywood siding on the county record

The front is brick, but the county lists 80 percent of the walls as plywood. Plywood panel siding rots at the bottom edges and around windows. Check the sides and back.

Cost: Spot repairs a few hundred to a few thousand; full re-siding $15,000 to $30,000 (estimate).

1969 plumbing and electrical

Ask about the drain line material and the panel. A sewer scope is worth it on a 57-year-old house.

Cost: Sewer scope $150 to $300.

Only 3 bedrooms

All three are on the main floor. The lower level has a family room and a bath but no bedroom.

Cost: A lower-level bedroom with an egress window runs about $8,000 to $15,000 (estimate).

Ask before making an offer

  1. Why did both October contracts fall through?
  2. Ages of the roof, furnace, air conditioner and water heater.
  3. What siding is on the sides and back, and any rot repairs?
  4. Has the sump pump ever run often, and any water in the lower level?
