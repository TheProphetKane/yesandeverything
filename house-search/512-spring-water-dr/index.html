Problems and risks

Investor flip, three months old

Quick flips get paint and carpet first, so look hard at what was not touched: furnace, air conditioner, water heater, windows, deck.

Cost: Mechanicals from 2001 could need $9,500 to $16,500 (estimate).

Lower-level finish bigger than the county record

The county shows 373 finished square feet, the listing says about 1,000. Ask for permits for the rest.

Cost: Unknown; matters to an appraiser.

Lower-level bedroom escape window

The lower bedroom windows look like small high basement windows (photo 18). A legal bedroom needs one large enough to climb out of.

Cost: An egress window runs $3,000 to $6,000 (estimate).

The contract that fell through

Under contract September 9, back October 6. Ask what the inspection found.

Cost: Unknown until asked.

Ask before making an offer

  1. What did the September buyer's inspection find?
  2. Ages of the furnace, air conditioner and water heater.
  3. Permits for the roof and the lower-level finish.
  4. Roof warranty, and does it transfer?
