Ask before making an offer

  1. Why as-is, and is there a seller's disclosure?
  2. Ages of the furnace, air conditioner, water heater and roof.
  3. How much of the basement is finished, and has it ever had water?
  4. Is the fence in good shape, and who owns the shared sections?
