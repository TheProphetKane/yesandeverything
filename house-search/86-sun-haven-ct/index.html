The short answer

A 2002 ranch in Whisper Creek, the same subdivision as 512 Spring Water Dr, with all three bedrooms on the main floor, a finished lower level with its own full bath, and nearly half an acre at the end of a cul-de-sac behind a wood fence. One owner since it was built. Fresh paint and new flooring throughout read as a seller getting it ready, not a renovation, so the kitchen, baths and mechanicals are probably 2002 originals. Forty-two days on the market with one small cut says the first price was a little high. The $42 association fee is as close to none as this search gets.
