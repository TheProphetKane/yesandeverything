How to read the plan

  • Family prices the materials, because the family supplies the labor: Nick, Lexi, Nick's dad Bill, his brothers Gannan and AJ, Obo, who is Grandma, plus their friend Dom. Where a contractor does the job either way, the family price is the contractor's. Hired prices the same job done by a contractor at Saint Louis rates, labor included.

  • Windows: the inspector's write-up of the rot goes on the credit request as repair value, and the family does the replacing its own way.
