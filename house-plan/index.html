House plan, if the purchase goes through

45 Autumn Crest Ct: the house plan

The family went under contract on Sunday, November 1, 2026, at the asking price of $330,000. The sellers close on their next house on Monday, November 30, so this one closes by then. This page is the plan from the inspection to the last basement room, if the inspection and the appraisal both clear.

Dates after closing assume a closing on Monday, November 30, and a move-in on Saturday, December 19, because the family spends November 22 to 29 away at Thanksgiving and an earlier closing signed ahead would leave the house waiting on them anyway. Every price is an estimate. The family column counts materials only, since Nick, Dad, Nick's brother and Grandma supply the labor, while the hired column prices the same job done by a contractor at St. Louis rates, labor included. Each table ends on its section total, and the estimated totals close the page.

Estimated cost, contract day to the last basement room: $29,800 to $45,100 with the family doing the work it can, or $66,500 to $123,600 hired out. Plan on up to $51,900 or $142,200 with a 15% cushion. The long-term list comes on top.
