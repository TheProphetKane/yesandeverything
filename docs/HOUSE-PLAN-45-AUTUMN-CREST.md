# 45 Autumn Crest Ct: the house plan

The family went under contract on Monday, November 2, 2026, at the asking price of $330,000. The sellers close on their next house on Monday, November 30, so this one closes by then. This page is the plan from the inspection to the last basement room, if the inspection and the appraisal both clear.

Dates after closing assume a closing on Monday, November 30, and a move-in on Saturday, December 19, because the family spends November 22 to 29 away at Thanksgiving and an earlier closing signed ahead would leave the house waiting on them anyway. Every price is an estimate. The family column counts materials only, since Kane, Dad, Kane's brother and Grandma supply the labor, while the hired column prices the same job done by a contractor at St. Louis rates, labor included.
