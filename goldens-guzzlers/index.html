    House rules

    ▾
    • Singles only, no packs. Cans or bottles are both fine.
    • No repeated styles in your own lineup, and one lager at most for every five ales.
    • Write your name on the bottom of every can and bottle.
    • Everything goes in one ice bucket, and straight into a coozie when it's pulled.
    • Pour 2 to 3 ounces each. Taste it, then everyone votes at once.
    • Thumbs up means you'd drink it again. Thumbs down means not for you. Sideways is rare.
    • Guess who brought it, then pull the coozie for the reveal.
    • One puller pulls every beer from the bucket. No dumping: finish what's poured.
